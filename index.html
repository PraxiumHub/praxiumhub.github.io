Praxium Hub takes its name from praxis and curriculum, reflecting our belief that real understanding comes from connecting theory with hands-on experience.

What began as a framework for practical education has grown into a studio dedicated to life-observation simulations — applications that turn curiosity into knowledge through patient, everyday practice.

Our first title

Crystal Shrimp Tank

Coming soon to Google Play

A quiet 3D aquarium for raising Crystal Red and Crystal Black shrimp. Keep the water healthy, aquascape your tank, and watch generations unfold — each shrimp carrying genes that shape its pattern, grade, and the offspring it may one day have.

  • Genetics & grading

    Gene-based inheritance drives every pattern. Breed from grade C up to SSS, and chase rare hidden morphs.

  • A real life cycle

    Shrimp grow, molt, court and breed. Watch the mating dance, follow the eggs, and welcome the hatchlings.

  • Living water

    Ammonia, minerals, temperature and pH all shift over time. Plants help; neglect shows. Balance is the craft.

  • Aquascaping

    Place Anubias, Java Fern, mosses and lotus; arrange driftwood and stone; set the light and backdrop to your mood.

  • Made for gazing

    Front, top, observe and inside-the-tank cameras. Slow pacing and few chores — an aquarium you can simply sit with.

  • Photo mode

    Capture your favorite moments, save them to your gallery, and share the tank you’ve grown.
